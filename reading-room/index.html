The Reading Room.

Stay a little longer.
Bring a friend. Talk about the journey.

Original discussion prompts to explore after reading.
Some questions refer to the story’s central events.

01The most unlikely companion

What draws you to stories where unlikely friends must rely on each other? How does the friendship between the gnome and the bird shape your reading of the journey?

02The things we carry

The gnome carries something important without understanding its significance. How does that idea change the way you think about his role?

03A difficult road

What do Laurelwood’s obstacles reveal about her choices and priorities? Which moment made you reconsider her situation?

04What light means to you

How does the struggle between light and darkness work beyond the fantasy setting? What does hope look like in the story?

05After the final page

Which companion stayed with you most? What would you ask Laura about the world she created?
