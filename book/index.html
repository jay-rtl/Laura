Dawn’s Prevail.

By Laura Mountainspring

The Search for the Light Scroll

A world at the edge
of darkness.

A gnome and a lost bird form an unexpected friendship. Joined by magical companions and a giant hare, they set out for Mount Kadosh. Unbeknownst to the gnome, something in his pocket could protect their world from darkness.

Laurelwood distracts her wicked mother so the companions can escape. But her own path to the mountain is threatened by royal councils, deception, and her half brother’s interference.

As their paths converge, friendship and difficult choices become part of a struggle with consequences for the entire world.
