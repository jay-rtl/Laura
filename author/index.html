About Laura

Creativity takes many forms.

Laura holds an associate degree in theology and enjoys learning about cultures and their mythologies. Alongside writing, her interests include painting and songwriting.

Her fantasy novel, Dawn’s Prevail, follows unlikely companions whose journey becomes part of their world’s struggle against darkness.
